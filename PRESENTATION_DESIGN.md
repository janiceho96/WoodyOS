# Woody Dark Premium Presentation Design System

## Brand personality

Premium, intelligent, calm, modern, and slightly futuristic. Avoid generic corporate templates, crowded slides, clip art, and excessive gradients.

## Deck style

- Style: premium-dark

Available styles: `premium-dark`, `minimal-light`, `strategy-dashboard`, `investor-pitch`, and `research-report`.

## Colors

- Background: #0B1020
- Surface: #121A30
- Primary: #7C5CFF
- Accent: #4DE1FF
- Success: #4EE3A3
- Primary text: #F5F7FF
- Secondary text: #AAB3CC

## Typography

- Headings: Aptos Display
- Body font: Aptos
- Maximum 35 words on a normal content slide.

## Visual direction

Use generous whitespace, subtle cards, thin borders, clear hierarchy, and diagrams over long bullet lists. Prefer one core idea per slide. Use short comparisons, frameworks, process steps, and tables only when exact information is required.

## Avoid

- Default PowerPoint layouts
- Tiny text
- Long paragraphs
- More than two fonts
- Busy backgrounds
- Decorative visuals with no meaning
